# 2026‒08‒27

This piece is surely inspired by Joe Hisaishi's Bygone Days from the movie Porco Rosso.
It also feels quite Pokémon-esque. Like something I could imagine playing in some of the cities you visit in the games (though the loop is  little too short).
This piece gave me a harder time. The rhythm especially, as it took me some time to figure out, that what I was writing fits into $\frac{12}{8}$.

# 2026‒08‒28

*A friend of mine has set a goal to write a poem a day. He's done pretty admirably in my opinion.
He was however lamenting how bad some of his work is, given that he often writes these poems dead tired at the end of the day.
As you might have seen from the dates of my pieces, I usually take my time.
Today though, I felt like following his example and wrote a piece in one day. Just for the sake of it.
Making art in just a day is hard... (less time to marinate and think things through)*

The chords in this one feel inspired by [Xi](https://soundcloud.com/xi_com_giko_31) and [David Peacock](https://www.daviddpeacock.com/)'s Piano work.
